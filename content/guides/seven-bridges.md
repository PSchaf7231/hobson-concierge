# Living in Seven Bridges

Seven Bridges is the largest of GL Homes' "Bridges" communities west of Delray Beach, with about 700 single-family homes behind a guarded gate on Lyons Road. Its clubhouse and tennis center are among the biggest on the corridor.

If you are shopping the **$1M+** band out west, the question is not whether Seven Bridges has enough amenities. It is whether you will use them, and whether the home and lot you can get here beat what the same budget buys closer to the water.

## Location & access

Seven Bridges sits on the west side of Lyons Road, between Clint Moore Road and Atlantic Avenue, directly across Lyons from its older sibling, The Bridges. It has a Delray Beach address and sits close to the Boca Raton line.

Atlantic Avenue is the obvious line east, running toward Delray Marketplace, downtown Delray and eventually the ocean. The Florida Turnpike runs just to the east, and I-95 is farther out. Boca's shopping and dining along Glades Road is the southern pull. This is inland living with a resort inside the gate. Before you commit, decide how often you really want to be at the beach or on Atlantic Avenue.

## Builder, era & homes

GL Homes began selling Seven Bridges in 2014, and the community was completed by 2019, with 701 single-family homes. The builder's sales are finished, so homes now trade as resales.

Homes are one- and two-story, in several collections. Published plans range from roughly 2,400 square feet to about 8,000 square feet under air, one of the widest ranges in a single community on the corridor. Many lots back to lakes or preserve. The larger estate plans on premium lots are where Seven Bridges meets the Anasa Collection.

## Gate, clubhouse & courts

Seven Bridges is guard-gated. The association describes a clubhouse and amenity complex of about 33,000 square feet. GL's own announcements put the clubhouse building itself at about 30,000.

**Clubhouse.** A full-service restaurant and bar, a lounge with a catering kitchen, card rooms, a game room and arcade, a toddler playroom, an indoor sports court, a fitness center with a group fitness studio, and locker rooms with saunas and massage rooms.

**Tennis.** The tennis center opened ahead of the clubhouse with twelve Har-Tru clay courts and one hard court, all lighted, plus shade pavilions, stadium-style seating, a pro shop and locker rooms. The association now also lists pickleball.

**Pools & outdoor.** A resort-style pool, a separate lap pool, a splash pad, and indoor and outdoor basketball.

There is no golf course inside the gates.

## How ownership works

Seven Bridges is a homeowners association community, not an equity club. The amenities are run by the association, which handles the gate, the grounds and the clubhouse. Before you make an offer, ask the association for its current budget, what the dues cover, any special assessments, and the status of any past or pending claims involving the common areas. Then read the documents, not only the summary.

## Frequently asked

**How is Seven Bridges different from The Bridges?**
They sit across Lyons Road from each other and were both built by GL Homes. The Bridges came first, starting in 2012, with 591 homes and a 17,000-square-foot clubhouse. Seven Bridges followed, with about 700 homes, a larger clubhouse and a bigger tennis center.

**How many tennis courts are there?**
Thirteen: twelve lighted Har-Tru clay courts and one hard court, according to GL's opening announcements. The association also lists pickleball courts.

**Is Seven Bridges gated?**
Yes. It is guard-gated, and the association runs entry for residents and guests.

**Is there golf?**
No golf course is inside the gates. Golfers usually pair a home here with a club membership elsewhere.

**Is it in Delray Beach or Boca Raton?**
It has a Delray Beach address, close to the Boca Raton line, in the western part of the county along Lyons Road.
