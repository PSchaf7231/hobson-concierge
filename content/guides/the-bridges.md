# Living in The Bridges

The Bridges is the first of GL Homes' luxury "Bridges" communities west of Delray Beach. It has 591 single-family homes behind a gate on Lyons Road, and you reach them by crossing water, which is where the name comes from. More than a decade in, it has the settled landscaping and established social calendar that newer communities are still growing into.

A note on the name: "The Bridges" is used by more than one community in Florida. This guide covers the GL Homes community in Delray Beach, Palm Beach County, across Lyons Road from Seven Bridges.

## Location & access

The Bridges sits on the east side of Lyons Road, between Clint Moore Road and Atlantic Avenue, with its entrance off Golden Gate Avenue. Seven Bridges is directly across Lyons Road. The community has a Delray Beach address and sits close to the Boca Raton line.

Atlantic Avenue runs east toward Delray Marketplace, downtown Delray and the beach. The Florida Turnpike runs nearby, and Glades Road and Town Center at Boca Raton are the pull to the south. This is inland, amenity-first living, so be honest with yourself about how often you want salt air before you choose the corridor.

## Builder, era & homes

GL Homes began building The Bridges in 2012. By early 2014 it had sold most of the community, and the clubhouse opened in December 2014. The association counts 591 homes across eight subdivisions. GL's sales are long finished, so every home is a resale.

Homes are one- and two-story single-family estates in several collections, and many lots look out on the lakes that run through the property. Because The Bridges is a few years older than its neighbors, some homes have been renovated and some are close to original. That spread is where careful touring pays off, and where homes reach into the Anasa Collection band.

## Gate, clubhouse & courts

The Bridges is a private, gated community. The association lists the following.

**Clubhouse.** A 17,000-square-foot clubhouse with a grand lounge (the Golden Gate Room), a multipurpose social hall and a catering kitchen. There is also a card room, a gaming lounge with arcade and table games, and a toddler room.

**Dining.** A full-service restaurant with indoor and covered patio seating. On-site dining like this is uncommon among gated communities in the area.

**Racquet Center.** Six lighted clay courts and four lighted pickleball courts, with shade pavilions, elevated viewing areas and a pro shop that handles stringing.

**Fitness & sports.** A fitness center open 24 hours, studios for yoga, Pilates and group classes, an indoor sports complex, and locker rooms with saunas.

**Pool & outdoor.** A resort-style pool and spa with shaded cabanas, a splash park, a wading pool, a playground and an outdoor basketball court.

There is no golf course inside the gates.

## How ownership works

The Bridges is a homeowners association community, with no equity club to join. Maintenance assessments are billed quarterly. The association collects a capital contribution of one quarter's assessment at closing on every home purchase, and any exterior change needs approval from the Architectural Control Committee or the board. Ask the association for current dues, what they cover, and any special assessments before you make an offer.

## Frequently asked

**When was The Bridges built?**
GL Homes began building in 2012, and the clubhouse opened in December 2014. The community is fully built out.

**How many homes are in The Bridges?**
591 single-family homes across eight subdivisions, according to the association.

**Is there a restaurant on site?**
Yes. The clubhouse includes a full-service restaurant with indoor and covered outdoor seating.

**What does a buyer pay at closing to the association?**
The association collects a capital contribution of one quarterly assessment at closing. Your closing statement will show the current figure.

**How does it compare with Seven Bridges?**
The Bridges is older and smaller, with 591 homes and a 17,000-square-foot clubhouse. Seven Bridges, across Lyons Road, is newer, with about 700 homes and a larger clubhouse and tennis center.
